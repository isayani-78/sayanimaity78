About Me

Sayani Maity is a Digital Forensics Enthusiast, Penetration Tester, Security Researcher, and VAPT & Application Security Analyst from Kolkata, and a graduate in Digital Forensics from the Indian School of Ethical Hacking (ISOEH), Kolkata, under Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology (MAKAUT), West Bengal, India.

As a CEH v13 AI Certified and CHFI v11 Certified cybersecurity professional, I specialize in Penetration Testing (WAPT/VAPT), Application Security, Digital Forensics, Cybercrime Investigation, OSINT, Vulnerability Assessment, and Security Research. I have hands-on experience with industry-standard tools including Nmap, Burp Suite, Autopsy, and advanced OSINT frameworks, along with strong proficiency in Python and Bash for security automation.

My work focuses on identifying vulnerabilities, analyzing attack surfaces, investigating digital evidence, and strengthening cyber defense through practical security research and automation. With a growing interest in AI-driven security, I explore intelligent detection and analysis solutions while continuously developing my skills through CTFs, deep-dive threat analysis, and real-world cybersecurity projects.
